1. Package Contents

This pack includes 6 unique low-poly models with 4 hand-painted themes (24 variations total):

Models: Sword, Axe, Shield, Spear, Bow, Staff.

Themes: Medieval, Fire , Ghost , Prism.


2. Technical Details

Polycount: Low-poly (1332 to 2908 tris per mesh).

Textures: 2048 x 2048 (BaseMap and Emission).

Materials: Prefabs are set up using all render pipelines. 

3. How to Use

Navigate to the Prefabs folder.

Drag and drop your desired weapon into your scene.

To change themes, simply swap the Material on the Mesh Renderer with one of the 4 included theme materials.

Note on Glow: To see the glowing effect, ensure your project has Bloom enabled in your Post-Processing volume!
